# Hallowed — Patch Notes

Full notes for each release are in [patchnotes/](patchnotes/).

## 1.0.0: The First Hallowed Night

**Supported platforms:** Minecraft 26.3 (Fabric, NeoForge)

The first release. See [patchnotes/1.0.0.md](patchnotes/1.0.0.md).

* **Hallowed Nights** in season (October 15 to November 5) and on one full moon in four: a violet sky, a Harvest
  Moon, embers, leaves and ambient sounds. **The Witching Hour** at midnight.
* **Trick-or-treating** with a Treat Pail, costumes, and six harmless tricks.
* **Eight candies** with real effects, Sugar Rush and the Sugar Crash.
* **Carved faces**: eight faces, lit and soul variants, wearable; lit jack o'lanterns ward off spirits.
* **Spirits** and **will-o'-the-wisps** that lead to Hallowed Caches.
* **The Hollow Horseman**, a two-phase boss, with the Reaper Scythe and the Horseman's Head.
* Witch Hat, a music disc, 14 advancements, `/hallowed` commands and commented configs.
