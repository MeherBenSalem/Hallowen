# Hallowed 1.0.0: The First Hallowed Night

**Supported platforms:** Minecraft 26.3 (Fabric, NeoForge) · Java 25 · Fabric API on Fabric

The first release.

## Hallowed Nights

- From October 15 to November 5 every night is a **Hallowed Night**; the rest of the year, one full moon in four.
  Seasonal, always, full moons only or manual, in the config.
- A violet sky, fog and light, a swollen orange **Harvest Moon**, drifting embers and autumn leaves, and owls, wind,
  chimes and whispers. A banner at dusk.
- **The Witching Hour** (midnight to 3 AM): twelve tolls of a bell, a banner, a vignette, and twice the spirits.
- Zombies and skeletons spawn in costume: carved pumpkins and lit jack o'lanterns with the new faces.

## Trick-or-treating

- **Treat Pail**: hold it out to a villager or wandering trader on a Hallowed Night. Treats go into the pail;
  a costume on your head earns more and makes treats likelier.
- Six harmless tricks, each one switchable: bat burst, pumpkin head, levitation, spook, vanishing act and sticky fingers.

## Candy

- Eight candies: Candy Corn, Caramel Apple, Ghost Gummy, Pumpkin Truffle, Witch's Lollipop, Bat Wing Licorice,
  Eyeball Jawbreaker and Soul Toffee, each with its own effect.
- **Sugar Rush** stacks into Sugar Rush II, then a **Sugar Crash**. New effects: Sugar Rush, Sugar Crash, Spooked
  and Spirit Ward.
- Monsters killed on a Hallowed Night drop candy, costumed ones always.

## Carved faces

- Shears carve eight faces into any pumpkin (sneak to go back); a torch lights it, a soul torch makes a Soul Jack
  o'Lantern. 24 wearable blocks.
- Lit jack o'lanterns, vanilla ones included, keep spirits away within 12 blocks (soul ones 20).

## Spirits and will-o'-the-wisps

- **Spirits** swoop through walls on Hallowed Nights and leave you Spooked. They drop Ectoplasm and fade at dawn.
- **Will-o'-the-wisps** lead you to a **Hallowed Cache**. Cursed (violet) wisps lead into an ambush with a
  better cache.

## The Hollow Horseman

- Wake a **Cursed Pumpkin** at the Witching Hour and the Horseman rises: a two-phase boss with his own boss bar
  and banners.
- Mounted: scythe sweeps, flaming jack o'lanterns, and charges you can stun against a wall. On foot below half
  health: he calls spirits and throws his own burning head.
- Drops the **Reaper Scythe** (Reap harvests and replants crops around you) or the **Horseman's Head** (Night
  Vision and Spirit Ward), Ectoplasm, a candy piñata, and sometimes the music disc or another Cursed Pumpkin.
- He rides away at dawn.

## Everything else

- **Witch Hat**, and **Music Disc: Witching Hour**, an original waltz.
- 14 advancements, `/hallowed` commands, and commented configs (`config/hallowed/hallowed.json` and `client.json`)
  with `/hallowed reload`.
- Every texture and the music were made for Hallowed, along with most of its sounds; around 30 sound events, all with
  subtitles.
